# SUSE Multi-Linux Manager (MLM) at Cloud Service Providers (CSPs)
A quick decision and overview guide for MLM at CSPs, formerly known as SUSE Manager (SUMa)

## Types of SUSE Multi-Linux Manager offerings at CSPs
* Both SUSE Multi-Linux Manager PAYG and SUSE Multi-Linux Manager BYOS are available from the Marketplaces

### SUSE Multi-Linux Manager PAYG

* When to use it?
** *Always, if you run in clouds*

* Where to get it?
** directly from the Amazon or Azure Marketplace. Unfortunately there is no offering for Google today.

* Why are there 2 listings and which one should I use?
** We have 2 listings for SUSE Multi-Linux Manager, "EU and UK only / EMEA Orders Only" and "non-EU and non-UK only", you should pick the listing that reflects in which country *your AWS & Azure account gets billed*

*** You may need to be logged in into the CSP portal in order to let the CSP system know your country of billing.

* Do i need subscriptions?
** No, its all automatic - no hassle with subscriptions

* I have SLES4SAP PAYG instances in the cloud, will i be billed for them?
** No, SUSE Multi-Linux Manager is aware of the embedded LCM subscriptions in the SLES4SAP PAYG offer.

* *What are the benefits?*
** Simplified purchasing (Cloud Spend on CSP)
** Pay only for what you use
** No subscription required
** Variable Monthly billing – based on peak usage
** EDP (Enterprise Discount Plan) / Committed Spend / MACC (Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitment)
** Automatic Subscription compliance
** Easier setup

* Sounds too good - What to be aware of?
** Billing starts from when the first managed client is on-boarded.
** To stop billing, delete clients from SUSE Multi-Linux Manager or shutdown SUSE Multi-Linux Manager instance
** *No ‘Migration’ from BYOS SUSE Multi-Linux Manager to PAYG version, you need to Redeploy, re-onboard clients*
** If you want to manage BYOS clients with SUSE Multi-Linux Manager PAYG you need to buy the BYOS subscriptions and set them up in SCC.

* Who does the support?
** As it is a SUSE offering - SUSE will provide L1-L3 support

* How do i get support?
** The PAYG offer has a link to https://scc.suse.com/cloudsupport, where you need to upload your supportconfig to open a ticket.
** For details please have a look at
    https://documentation.suse.com/suma/4.3/en/suse-manager/specialized-guides/public-cloud-guide/payg-support.html
    or
    https://documentation.suse.com/suma/5.0/en/suse-manager/specialized-guides/public-cloud-guide/payg-support.html

* Is there a FAQ?
** YES, look at
  https://documentation.suse.com/suma/4.3/en/suse-manager/specialized-guides/public-cloud-guide/public-cloud-faq.html
  or
  https://documentation.suse.com/suma/5.0/en/suse-manager/specialized-guides/public-cloud-guide/public-cloud-faq.html


### SUSE Multi-Linux Manager BYOS
* When to use it?
** If you used SUSE Multi-Linux Manager within the CSPs in the past

* Where to get it?
** directly from the Amazon, Azure and Google Marketplace.

* Do i need subscriptions?
** Yes, you need subscriptions for the SUSE Multi-Linux Manager Server itself and *need to buy LCM subscriptions for every managed client* (min. 10) from SUSE or a SUSE distributor.

* Who does the support?
** As it is a SUSE offering - SUSE will provide L1-L3 support

* How do i get support?
** Login and open your support ticket via your SCC account.

** The SUSE Multi-Linux Manager is supported directly by the worldwide SUSE Global Technical Support team or your assigned Premium Support Engineer.
This is true whether the SUSE Multi-Linux Manager subscription is purchased directly from SUSE or the Azure Marketplace.

** Please ensure your SUSE Multi-Linux Manager product is registered to your SUSE Customer Center (SCC) organization (https://scc.suse.com).

** Once logged in to the SCC, use the "Support" option under "My Tools" on the homepage to open a support case with SUSE. If you have any questions, a FAQ and a User Guide are available under the "Help" link on the landing page.
